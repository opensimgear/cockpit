## Acquisition and operation boundaries

- The companion serial worker retries correlated discovery before readiness. Status-only firmware is rejected there. A live status watch uses a one-second liveness ping. An eligible reconnect can request a fresh correlated snapshot after its recovery deadline. Portal and MCP status reads use the worker's committed state. [Direct USB verification](../../verification/2026-11-08-esp32-usb-status-watch-required.md)
- The Linux controller probe waits for evdev reports and udev topology events. The macOS probe uses IOHID input, matching and removal callbacks. Physical qualification of both adapters remains in #199.
- The qualification-only visibility child launch retains the 10–20 ms checks listed above. The live Windows visibility worker uses report, inventory and policy wakes. Its shared 250 ms executable audit also reads driver policy when registry watch coverage is unavailable.
- Virtual-controller refresh waits on the exact child process handle for completion and uses a separate wait after requested termination.
- The scanner finds named wait primitives. The STM32 module's `last_sample` comparison, the gateway's VBUS check and the Pico Debug loop are documented separately as board-side acquisition or transport service. [Board owners](README.md#acquisition-and-publication-without-a-named-timer-call)
